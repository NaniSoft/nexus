The factory has two human roles, and they are both real work.

- **[Writing an issue the factory can build](/docs/guides/writing-an-issue-the-factory-can-build)** —
  the issue is the factory's only specification. What a good one contains.
- **[Reviewing agent work on the Kanban](/docs/guides/reviewing-agent-work-on-the-kanban)** —
  the three decisions, what to look at before making them, and how the three
  round ceiling changes the conversation.

Neither role is optional. A factory with vague issues produces builds nobody
can approve; a factory whose reviews are rubber stamps is merging code nobody
read. The design gives the human three rounds precisely so the second of those
does not have to happen.
